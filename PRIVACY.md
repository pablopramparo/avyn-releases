# Avyn — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 October 2026
Publisher: Pablo Pramparo — contact: avyn.remote@gmail.com

## In short

Avyn lets you control your own Windows PC from your own Android phone over your local network. It
has two parts: the **Avyn Remote** app on the phone and **Avyn Agent** on the PC.

- Avyn has no accounts and no Avyn server. What the phone and the PC exchange goes directly
  between them, on your network, encrypted.
- Avyn has no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. The publisher does not receive
  your data.
- The only connection Avyn Agent makes to the Internet by itself is to check for and download its
  own updates (when it was installed from the Avyn download page; the Microsoft Store version
  doesn't connect to the Internet by itself).
- The only third party involved is Google, if you buy Avyn Premium in the Android app (see
  *Purchases*).

## What goes between your phone and your PC

All of it travels over an encrypted connection (TLS) between the two devices. The phone only
talks to a PC you have paired, and checks that PC's certificate on every connection; the PC only
accepts phones you approved on its screen, and you can revoke one at any time in Avyn Agent.

- **Pairing:** the name of the phone (the name you gave it in Android's settings, or its
  manufacturer and model), an identifier generated by the app, and a secret key created by the PC.
- **Control:** pointer movements, clicks, the text and keys you type, volume and media commands,
  and the commands for windows, displays, programs, power and routines.
- **What the PC shows the phone:** the titles and program names of open windows and their icons;
  what is playing (title, artist, album, artwork, position); the names of displays; the list of
  programs in the Start menu; and, if screen previews are on (they can be turned off in Avyn
  Agent), small screenshots of each display, which are not saved.
- **Files:** the names, sizes and dates of files in the folders you share, their thumbnails, and
  the content of the files you choose to send or download. By default only the standard personal
  folders are shared; you choose in Avyn Agent which folders, or turn file access off.
- **Finding the PC:** the phone sends a broadcast on the local network and the PC answers with
  its name, an identifier, its version and its port. This is not encrypted and anyone on the same
  network can see that an Avyn Agent is there.
- **Waking the PC:** the phone sends a standard Wake-on-LAN packet with the PC's network card
  address.

## What Avyn keeps on the phone

In Android's encrypted app storage (Keystore-backed), until you forget the PC or uninstall the app:

- for each paired PC: its name, local address and port, certificate fingerprint, the pairing
  secret, its network card address for waking it, and how to reach its unlock service;
- the app's own identifier;
- your settings (language, pointer speed, favourite and recent programs, last folder used);
- **if you set up unlocking:** the PIN or password of your Windows account, stored behind your
  fingerprint or screen lock. It is sent only to your PC's unlock service, when you ask to unlock.

Files you download from the PC are saved in the phone's Downloads/Avyn folder and stay there until
you delete them. Artwork and thumbnails are kept in the app's cache.

## What Avyn keeps on the PC

In your Windows user profile (`%LocalAppData%\Avyn`), until you uninstall or delete it:

- the Agent's certificate and the list of paired phones (name, identifier, pairing secret, dates),
  protected with Windows' data protection for your user;
- your settings, display names, shared folders and routines;
- if you pair a Samsung TV: its address, model and the access token the TV issued, protected the
  same way;
- **logs** of the last 14 days, for troubleshooting: connections, the names of paired phones, the
  names of files transferred and programs opened, and errors. They stay on the PC.

If you turn on unlocking from the phone, a separate Windows service keeps, in a folder only
Windows and administrators can read: its certificate, and for each phone allowed to unlock, the
phone's name, identifier and a key derived from the pairing secret; and a log of unlock attempts
(the phone's identifier, its local address and the result). **Your Windows PIN or password is
never stored on the PC.**

## Connections to the Internet and to other devices

- **Updates (Avyn Agent, direct download only):** checks GitHub
  (`github.com/pablopramparo/avyn-releases`) for a new version and downloads it. GitHub receives
  the PC's IP address, as any website does. The Microsoft Store version is updated by the Store
  and makes no connection of its own.
- **Samsung TV (optional):** to find and control a TV, Avyn Agent looks for it on your local
  network and connects to the TV itself. Nothing leaves your network.
- **Links you tap** (the Microsoft Store page of Auren, the Avyn Agent download page, the support
  e-mail address) open in your browser or mail app.
- The phone app does not connect to any Internet service by itself.

## Browser extension

The Avyn extension for Chrome and Edge tells Avyn Agent, on the same PC, about tabs that are
playing video: the site's name, the tab and media titles, playback state and which player
controls are available. It sends this only to Avyn Agent on your PC, never over the network. It
has its own policy: [PRIVACY-browser-extension.md](PRIVACY-browser-extension.md).

## Diagnostics

Avyn sends nothing by itself. If you choose "Export diagnostics", Avyn creates a text with
versions, settings and recent activity, written so that it contains no device names, addresses,
file names, PINs or keys, and you decide whether and where to send it.

## Purchases

Avyn Remote is free to download. Some features need **Avyn Premium**, a one-time purchase made in
Google Play. There is no subscription and no Avyn account.

- Payment is handled entirely by Google Play. Avyn never sees your card or payment details.
- Google Play tells the app whether this Google account owns Premium. Avyn keeps that purchase
  record, exactly as Google Play provides it, in the phone's encrypted app storage so Premium keeps
  working without an Internet connection, and checks Google Play again when the app starts and
  when you return to it.
- To enable Premium features on your PC, the app shows that purchase record to your own Avyn Agent,
  over the same encrypted connection as everything else. It does not leave your network. Avyn
  Agent checks it on the PC and does not contact anyone about it. It does not keep the purchase
  record after the connection ends; if unlocking from the phone is on, the unlock service
  remembers only a yes or no, per phone, for whether that phone has Premium.
- The publisher does not receive purchase information from the app. Google may give the
  publisher sales reports in the Google Play Console, as for any app.

## Sharing

Avyn does not sell or share your data. The publisher does not receive it.

## Children

Avyn is a tool for controlling a personal computer and is not directed at children.

## Your choices

- Revoke a phone: Avyn Agent → Devices. Forget a PC: in the app, in the list of PCs.
- Turn off file access, screen previews or program launching: Avyn Agent → Settings.
- Remove the unlock PIN: in the app, where you set it. Turn unlocking off: Avyn Agent → Settings.
- Delete everything: uninstall the app and Avyn Agent, and delete `%LocalAppData%\Avyn` on the PC.
